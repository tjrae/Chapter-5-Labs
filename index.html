About Groove & Grain

We specialize in curated vintage vinyl: every record is inspected, cleaned, and play-graded when needed. We use Goldmine standards for grading and ship with corner protection, poly sleeves, and moisture barriers.

Shipping & Returns

  • Flat-rate domestic shipping: $6 (free over $100)
  • International available—rates at checkout upon request
  • Returns within 14 days for grading discrepancies; buyer pays return shipping
  • Orders ship in 1-2 business days with tracking

FAQ

How do you grade records?

We follow Goldmine standards (M, NM, EX, VG+, VG, G). Jackets and vinyl are graded separately. We note matrix/runout and pressing details when relevant.

Do you clean records?

Yes—dry brushed and spot cleaned. Audiophile ultrasonic cleaning is available on request.

Can I request a specific pressing?

Absolutely! Send us your wishlist via the contact form below, and we'll source it.
